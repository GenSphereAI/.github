# GenSphere AI

**Shenzhen Weijing Nishe Technology Co., Ltd.**

We are building AI-native interactive storytelling and role-playing games through world models, multimodal systems, and persistent memory architectures.

---

## What We Do

At GenSphere, our research spans **world models**, **language models**, and **multimodal models** — with a focus on unifying these capabilities into coherent, long-running interactive systems.

We are not just asking whether AI can generate content. We are asking whether AI can:
- **Understand** a world continuously
- **Remember** what has happened within it
- **Preserve consistency** across characters and environments
- **Advance stories** through long-term interaction

Our goal is to build worlds that exist and evolve over time: characters with long-term memory, events with causal continuity, relationships that accumulate, environments that change, and player decisions that meaningfully shape the future.

---

## Core Technology Stack

- **Long-context management** & persistent memory
- **Memory classification, compression, & retrieval**
- **Character state** & relationship modeling
- **Topic management** & world-state management
- **Event systems** with causal continuity
- **Multimodal generation** (language, image, video, world models)

We research **memory compression**, **hierarchical memory**, **dynamic context selection**, **structured state representation**, **caching**, and **model routing** to make persistent virtual worlds feasible at scale.

---

## Why Games First

Games are where we place these capabilities into users hands first. We aim to build a new computational medium in which worlds themselves can be generated, understood, remembered, and changed.
